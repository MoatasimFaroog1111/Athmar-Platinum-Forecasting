# Data integrity gates

WPIC raw cells are audit evidence only; official metric names, period, unit and release-vintage need mapping before joining price features.

CFTC Tuesday position dates are distinct from release timestamps. A source-verified calendar is mandatory; the existing conservative seven-day offset is NOT an actual publication time.

Macroeconomic releases must carry period_end, available_at, value, source and unit. Unverified sources are blocked.

Yahoo PL=F is indicative futures, not a licensed spot price. Roll policy and vendor terms must be reviewed.

A future daily baseline is not a validated forecast and provisional historical residual quantiles cannot establish calibrated coverage to December 2027.

No trading, merge or deployment is authorized by these changes.
